What does an oxygen conserver do?

A conserver attaches to your oxygen cylinder and controls how oxygen is released. Instead of a constant stream, the Bonsai Velocity sends oxygen right at the start of each breath, when it is most effective. This targeted delivery helps reduce waste, so your cylinder can last longer between refills.

Attach to your cylinder

Slide the unit onto a CGA 870 post-valve cylinder so the two alignment pins fit the holes on the post valve, then tighten the cylinder adjustment knob.

2

Choose your setting

Turn the selector switch to the pulse setting your provider recommends, or to cf 2 or cf 4 for continuous flow.

3

Breathe normally

Oxygen is delivered at the start of each breath. Check the gauge to see how much remains, and turn the switch to OFF when not in use.

Compatibility: designed for CGA 870 post-valve cylinders. Liter flow 1–6. For full setup and safety instructions, see the owner's manual.
